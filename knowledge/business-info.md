# About Northgate Dental

Northgate Dental is a family dental practice at 42 Northgate Street, Riverbend.
We have been treating patients in the area since 2009. The practice has three
dentists, two hygienists, and a small front-desk team.

# Opening hours and location

We are open Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. We are closed on weekends and public
holidays. The last routine appointment of the day starts at 4:30pm.

We are on Northgate Street, two doors down from the Riverbend Post Office. There
is free patient parking behind the building, entered from Mill Lane. The nearest
bus stop is Northgate Square, served by the 14 and the 22.

The practice is on the ground floor and is fully step-free. There is an
accessible toilet and a hearing loop at the front desk.

# Services and appointment lengths

- Routine check-up and scale — 30 minutes
- Hygienist appointment — 30 minutes
- New patient consultation — 45 minutes
- Filling — 45 minutes
- Root canal treatment — 90 minutes, usually across two visits
- Tooth extraction — 45 minutes
- Teeth whitening consultation — 30 minutes
- Emergency appointment — 20 minutes, same day where possible

We do not offer orthodontics or dental implants in-house. We refer those cases to
Riverbend Orthodontic Centre.

# Fees

- Routine check-up — 55
- Hygienist appointment — 65
- New patient consultation including X-rays — 95
- Filling — from 120, depending on size and material
- Root canal — from 450 for a front tooth, from 650 for a molar
- Extraction — from 150
- Teeth whitening — 320 for the full course
- Emergency appointment — 80, deducted from the cost of any treatment that follows

Fees are guide prices. The dentist confirms the exact cost after examining the
patient. We do not quote a final price over the phone.

# Payment and insurance

We accept card, cash and bank transfer. Payment is taken on the day of treatment.

We are registered with Denplan and accept most major dental insurance plans.
Patients claim back from their insurer directly; we provide an itemised receipt.
We offer interest-free payment plans over six months for treatment above 500.

# New patients

We are currently accepting new patients. A new patient consultation takes 45
minutes and includes a full examination, X-rays, and a written treatment plan.
Please arrive ten minutes early to complete a medical history form, or ask us to
email the form in advance.

# Cancellations

Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or move an appointment. Missed
appointments without notice are charged at 30. We send an SMS reminder two days
before every appointment.

# Emergencies

For severe pain, swelling that affects breathing or swallowing, or an injury
after an accident, patients should go to A&E or call emergency services rather
than wait for an appointment.

For a knocked-out adult tooth: hold it by the crown, not the root, rinse it in
milk if dirty, put it back in the socket if possible, and get to us within the
hour. Call as soon as you are on your way.

Out of hours, the on-call service number is on our answerphone message.
