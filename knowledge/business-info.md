# Reservations

We take reservations for any group size — no minimum or maximum. A
reservation only needs a name and the date/time requested; a phone number
isn't required to hold a table. The owner has the table ready at the
requested time.

# Takeout and delivery

Both are available. Delivery runs through Swiggy and Zomato; pickup orders
can be placed directly by phone.
